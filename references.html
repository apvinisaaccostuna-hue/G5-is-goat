Sources & references

These sources provide background on consumption taxes, household income, import duties, and how prices change.

  1. OECD. Consumption Tax Trends 2022: VAT/GST and Excise, Core Design Features and Trends. doi.org/10.1787/6525a942-en
  2. Congressional Budget Office. The Distribution of Household Income, 2019. Background on household income and distributional analysis. cbo.gov/publication/58353
  3. U.S. International Trade Commission. Economic Effects of Trade Agreements Implemented Under Trade Authorities Procedures, 2016 Report. Includes discussion of tariff effects and pass-through evidence. usitc.gov/publications/332/pub4614.pdf
  4. U.S. Federal Reserve. Inflation: What It Is, Why It Matters, and How It Is Measured. An overview of inflation and purchasing power. federalreserve.gov/faqs/economy_14419.htm
This guide is for education, not tax, legal, or investment advice. Tax rules and effects vary by jurisdiction and may change over time.
