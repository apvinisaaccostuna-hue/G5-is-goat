Understanding the issue · FCS

The problem

Consumers may not realize that some taxes are already included in the prices of products and services.

Explanation of the problem by Alshekeiah Pilapil

Explore the 5 Whys ↓

The five whys

Answer by Matthew Zenchi Farala

WHY 1

Why do consumers sometimes pay hidden taxes without noticing them?

Because the taxes may already be included in the prices of products instead of being displayed as separate charges.

WHY 2

Why are these taxes not always shown separately?

Because some businesses display tax-inclusive prices, meaning the price customers see already includes the applicable tax.

WHY 3

Why can this make it difficult for consumers to understand product prices?

Because consumers may focus only on the final price without knowing how much of it represents taxes or how taxes affect the product's cost.

WHY 4

Why might consumers not understand how taxes affect prices?

Because they may have limited knowledge about different types of taxes, how businesses account for them, and how they can affect selling prices.

WHY 5

Why is improving tax awareness important?

Because understanding taxes helps consumers make informed purchasing decisions, manage their budgets, and understand the factors that affect the prices of everyday products.

What is driving the issue?

Root cause

Limited consumer awareness and understanding of how taxes are presented and how they can affect product prices.

Steps toward clearer choices

Proposed solution

Businesses should provide clear and accurate price information, while schools and educational programs should teach consumers about taxes and budgeting. Consumers can also check receipts, compare prices, and use reliable sources to learn about taxes.
