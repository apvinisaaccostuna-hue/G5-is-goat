About hidden taxes

Hidden taxes are already included in the prices of things we buy instead of being shown separately. Sometimes we do not notice that we are paying them because they are already part of the price.

What is our topic anyway?

These taxes can make everyday things like food, clothes, gas, and imported products more expensive. Since the tax may be included earlier in the production or supply process, it might not appear as a separate line on a price tag or receipt.

What are hidden taxes? Hidden taxes are taxes included in the prices of products and services instead of being shown separately. Consumers pay them when they buy things, sometimes without realizing it.
